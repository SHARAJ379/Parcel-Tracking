Privacy policy

What this service collects, who receives it, and how to ask for it to be removed.

Last updated 27 November 2026. This is a university project and this policy has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

What we collect

There are no visitor accounts or sign-up forms. The service stores:

  • Parcel records: tracking ID, status, expected delivery time, destination, and timeline events (description, location, time). Parcels imported from email also store the courier, order ID, courier tracking number, item names and delivery city.
  • Email import log: time, result, reason, the sender's domain, a one-way hash of the email's Message-ID and the tracking IDs found. The email itself, its subject, the full sender address, phone numbers, street addresses and payment details are not stored.
  • Rate-limit counters: a one-way SHA-256 hash of your IP address, or the ID of an API key, with a request count per minute. Your IP address itself is not stored.
  • API keys: only a hash and a short prefix of each key, never the key itself. The administrator also stores the key name and scopes.
  • Webhooks: the endpoint URL, its signing secret, and a log of each delivery attempt (payload and the response received).

The assistant

When you ask the assistant a question, your message, up to ten earlier messages from the same conversation, and the records of the parcel you asked about are sent to Google's Gemini service to produce a reply. Chat messages are not saved in our database. Do not type personal details that are not needed to look up a parcel.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Administrators receive a signed session cookie that lasts eight hours. It is used only for sign-in and is not sent for visitors.
  • Your light or dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

There is no advertising or analytics tracking.

Who receives data

  • Supabase: stores the database.
  • Vercel: hosts the site and keeps standard request logs, such as time, page and IP address.
  • Google Gemini: receives assistant questions and parcel records as described above, and the text of forwarded or pasted parcel emails, with email addresses, phone numbers and card endings masked first.
  • CloudMailin: receives emails forwarded to the import address and passes them to this service.
  • Google Fonts: your browser loads fonts from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • Webhook endpoints: HTTPS URLs registered by an API user receive parcel status updates for that user's parcels.

Data is not sold.

How long data is kept

Parcel records, webhook logs and rate-limit counters stay in the database until the administrator deletes them. There is no automatic deletion yet. Vercel keeps its own logs for the period set by its plan.

Deletion and questions

To ask for a parcel record or other data to be removed, or to ask what is held about a tracking ID, contact the project owner, Sharaj R Shetty, and include the tracking ID. Under laws such as the GDPR and India's DPDP Act you may also ask to see or correct your data.
